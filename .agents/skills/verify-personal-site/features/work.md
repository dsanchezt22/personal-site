# Project board

At 760px and wider, #work is a dotted board of tilted, taped, double-bordered cards (one detailed ink-and-wash drawing, one-word category, title, one-line description). Hovering or focusing a card deepens its colour and runs one small movement in its drawing (packets hop on the memory mesh, the LED flashes, keys press, wheels turn, the display changes reading, pads light, a commit heads for main, a sofa drops into the room, dots flow to the cup). Cards drag with mouse or touch inside the board; a drag is not a click and opens nothing. A plain click opens that project's native dialog, which shows the same drawing at full size above the text. Below 760px the board is a plain stacked list with no dragging, so touch scrolling is never trapped.

Nine cards in three rows: Memory for AI inference (Research, #research, past tense: the research ended summer 2026), Typing-force energy harvester (Research, #piezo), ErgoTouch, A bike rebuilt (#ebike), Logic you can touch, RMX-1000-style effects box (Hardware, #rmx, in progress), Open-source contributions (Software, #developer), Realization Engine (AI, #realization), FStea AI (AI, #verdnt). The open-source dialog links the two open cmux pull requests, cmux, and Pi, and claims nothing more than "contributor" for Pi. The Realization Engine dialog links https://realization.verdntgroup.com/ and describes only what that site shows. The winery demos, Plate Scanner, and Course notebook are gone and must not come back.

Drive a real drag (`chrome-devtools-axi drag @card @target`) and confirm the card's `--x/--y` changed and no dialog opened; then click a card and confirm its dialog opens. Tab to a card and press Enter to open it. Close with the Close button, Escape, and a backdrop click; focus returns to the card. Check dialogs at 320 and 390 pixels.
