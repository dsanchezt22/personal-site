# Contact and socials

GitHub, X, LinkedIn, Email, and Resume appear in the hero and in #contact. X and LinkedIn hrefs come from the `SOCIALS` constant at the top of main.js (the HTML LinkedIn href is the no-JavaScript fallback). The email link uses mailto without a form.

Verify destinations and open the PDF. The HTTP check compares the served PDF byte-for-byte to the checked-in resume. Check the email is unclipped at 320 pixels. Do not activate mailto during automated verification.

The linked PDF must match the owner-supplied Resume_DiegoSanchezTirado.pdf (2026-10-28 update), published at the stable Diego-Sanchez-Tirado-Resume.pdf URL.
