# Background

#about ("background") opens with a wide drawing of a sandstone arcade, tower, and palms, then the short biography, then the experience circuit (#experience): six component boards for Verdnt, Stanford research, Amazon Lab126, Code Four, Duke, and Stanford education (#education). The Robust Systems Group entry is past ("2026 · through summer") and the biography says he researched there through summer 2026. Compare the other role names and dates against the supplied resume.

Each board is a native `<details>`: its summary shows a small drawn mark, the dates, the name, an LED, and a slide switch; opening it unfolds a detailed drawing and the description. At 760px and wider the boards alternate left and right of a copper trace; below that they stack to the right of it.

main.js draws the trace from the boards' real positions. As the page scrolls, the trace turns gold from the top down to a line 62% of the way down the viewport, with a blue spark at its tip. Until the spark reaches a board it is unpowered: grey frame, dark LED, and a hatched cover where its drawing and description go. When the spark arrives the board powers on (LED lights, mark gains colour, a short wobble) and its contents unfold in place. Boards keep their full size either way, so the page never changes height on scroll. Scrolling back up powers boards off again. Hovering or focusing a board tilts it and runs a small movement in its drawing.

Drive: scroll slowly through the section and confirm boards power on in order and the page height stays constant; click "projects" in the header from the top of the page and confirm it lands on #work; click a summary and confirm it closes and reopens; Tab to a summary and toggle it with Enter; load `/#education` and confirm that board is in view with all six on. With reduced motion (`--force-prefers-reduced-motion`) every board is on and open, the whole trace is gold, and there is no spark. Without JavaScript every board is open and a dashed line stands in for the trace.
