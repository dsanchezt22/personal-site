# Motion and sound controls

Motion on is the continuous workspace journey. Motion off returns to the same chapter in an ordinary readable document, with all chapters accessible and the canvas hidden. Scroll elsewhere in that layout, turn motion back on, and require the corresponding chapter rather than the previous one. OS reduced motion bypasses the intro and world creation, disables the control, and uses the readable document. A preference change is also handled. WebGL failure uses the same fallback.

Sound starts off. Sound on enables a short synthesized chime on chapter navigation. Toggle it off to suspend audio. Check the pressed states and console errors. A hidden tab suspends sound; audio resumes on the next relevant user click. If audio is unsupported, show Sound unavailable. Do not claim audio quality from a screenshot or pressed state.
