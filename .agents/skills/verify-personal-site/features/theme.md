# Motion and sound controls

The old dark/light theme control has been replaced by independent motion and sound controls. The palette is intentional and fixed.

Toggle Motion off: aria-pressed becomes false, the label becomes Motion off, and pointer/scroll movement and transitions stop. Turn it on to restore effects. The operating system's reduced-motion preference starts with motion disabled and bypasses the entrance; a preference change is also handled.

Sound starts off. Clicking Sound on enables a short synthesized glass chime on navigation and entry. Toggle it off to suspend audio. Check both pressed states and console errors. A hidden tab suspends sound; audio resumes only on the next relevant user click. If audio is unsupported, show Sound unavailable. Browser evidence does not substitute for listening to audio quality.
