# Personal portfolio

Diego Sanchez Tirado's portfolio at diegosancheztirado.com introduces his Stanford electrical engineering, research, hardware, and software work. GitHub, LinkedIn, and his current resume are prominent. Verdnt Group is one part of his experience, not the site's identity.

On 2026-11-17 Diego asked for the simple hand-drawn look of toryn.bio without its animation: one-word section titles (background, projects, contact) with an objective description line instead of taglines, a draggable project board whose cards open details on click, and X, LinkedIn, Email, and Resume linked. The monogram stays "dst". See docs/adr/004-drawn-simple-site.md, which supersedes the WebGL workspace of ADRs 002 and 003.

He found the first draft far too plain and asked for the example site's level of granular detail, still dynamic, with significant effort in the graphics. The drawings are therefore dense and original: a routed circuit board in the hero, one detailed ink-and-wash drawing per project, a campus strip, a contact still-life, schematic rules, and timeline marks, all on paper-textured off-white with hand-wobbled lines. See docs/adr/005-generated-drawings.md. He confirmed X as https://x.com/hnsmsquidwrd and LinkedIn as https://www.linkedin.com/in/diego-sanchez-tirado.

He confirmed Verdnt Group as the company name and asked to feature CMUX and Pi coursework for the Modern Software Developer course. He explicitly approved building and verifying directly in Codex after Firstmate startup failed.

Sources: the current Resume_DiegoSanchezTirado.pdf supplied on 2026-10-28; the existing public portfolio; CS146S assignment writeups summarized without private data; confirmed public winery demos; upstream CMUX and Pi repositories. Tool links do not claim authorship or merged contributions. Private course submissions and client internals are not published.

Runtime: static HTML, CSS, and a small main.js bundled by esbuild, with no runtime dependency. The drawings are generated by scripts/art at build time and inlined into dist/index.html where index.html has `<!--art:name-->` placeholders. Native dialogs hold project details. The project board drags with pointer events at 760px and wider and becomes a plain list below that. X and LinkedIn URLs come from the SOCIALS constant in main.js. Without JavaScript all section text remains readable. The explicit build allowlist excludes source notes and unused artwork from deployment. The existing Cloudflare Worker and domains remain the target.

Acceptance requires dragging a card without opening it, clicking and keyboard-opening every dialog, and checking responsive layouts. See the project verification skill.
